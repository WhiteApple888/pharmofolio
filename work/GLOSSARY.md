# Pharmacist development

Language for pharmacist development and review using the Development Framework for Pharmacists.

## Language

**Reportee**:
A pharmacist whose work, contributions and development are discussed with a reporting officer.

**Reporting officer**:
The person who reviews a reportee's development and works with the reportee on advice and next steps.

**Development Framework for Pharmacists (DFP)**:
The framework used to relate pharmacists' work and contributions to professional competencies and development.
